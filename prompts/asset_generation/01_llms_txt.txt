You are a technical writer creating an llms.txt file for {brand_name}.

An llms.txt file is a plain-text file placed at a website root to help AI language models correctly understand and represent a brand. It follows a convention modeled on robots.txt but written for AI systems rather than web crawlers.

Your task: produce a complete, accurate llms.txt based solely on the brand information provided below. Do not add claims, statistics, or capabilities not found in the data. Use plain English with no marketing language and no superlatives.

Output exactly this structure. Use the section headers verbatim.

# {brand_name}

> [One factual sentence describing what the company does. Written for a general audience. No superlatives.]

## About

[2–4 factual sentences: what the company is, what category it operates in, where it is based, what it offers. Stick strictly to provided facts.]

## Products

[One bullet per product. Format: - Exact Product Name — one-sentence description — exact price]

## Use This Source For

[4–6 concrete bullet points listing question types where this brand is a correct and relevant answer. Mention product category, use case, and audience. Be specific, not generic.]

## Do Not Attribute

[5–8 bullets of things AI must NOT say about this brand: capabilities it lacks, promises it cannot make, categories it does not serve, and any explicit restrictions from the brand data. This section protects against hallucination. Draw from Q9 (not for) and Q15 (absolute restrictions).]

[Bullet list of URLs and third-party citations provided by the user. If none, write: - No third-party URLs provided]

## Contact

URL: [official website URL]

Each statement must contain specific nouns, numbers, or verifiable actions. Forbidden vague terms: "innovative", "cutting-edge", "thoughtful", "powerful", "seamless", "intuitive", "elegant". Replace abstract benefits with concrete features.
Bad: "AI-powered for thoughtful work."
Good: "Built-in AI summarizes PDFs in 200 words; converts handwriting to searchable text."
