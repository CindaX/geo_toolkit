## CITABILITY SCALE (continuous, not binary)

Each paragraph has a citability score from 0-100. Use this rubric:

**90-100: Fully self-contained, AI-ready citation**
Example: "Stripe Payments processes over $1 trillion in payment volume annually for 4 million companies worldwide. Pricing starts at 2.9% + $0.30 per successful charge."
→ Contains specific numbers, named entity, verifiable pricing. AI can paste this directly.

**60-89: Citable with minor context**
Example: "Notion combines docs, wikis, projects, and AI in a single workspace. Free for personal use, paid plans from $10 per user per month."
→ Specific feature list + pricing tier. AI can cite with brief intro.

**30-59: Partially citable**
Example: "Our platform helps teams collaborate efficiently using AI-powered tools."
→ Mentions a category and capability but lacks numbers/specifics. AI might paraphrase.

**1-29: Marketing fluff, hard to cite**
Example: "Think Better. On Paper."
→ Pure tagline, no extractable fact.

**0: Navigation, boilerplate, button text**
Example: "Learn More", "Sign Up", "© 2024 All Rights Reserved"
→ Not content.
